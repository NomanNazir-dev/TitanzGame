FAQ

Questions studios ask us

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we quote each project after a short call or a look at your design doc. Smaller jobs, like a bug fix or a single feature, get one fixed price up front. Ports and full games are quoted per milestone, so you only commit to one stage at a time.

Do you only work in Unity?

Yes. Unity is our engine, and we know it deeply: rendering pipelines, XR, multiplayer, mobile monetization and editor tooling. If your game is in another engine, we'll tell you straight away rather than learn on your budget.

Where are you, and what hours do you work?

We're based in Pakistan (UTC+5). Our working day overlaps with UK and European business hours, and we schedule calls for North American mornings.

Who owns the code?

You do. When a milestone is paid, its source code and assets are yours, and you get the full Unity project at handover.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours before you share anything confidential.

How do payments work?

Per milestone, by bank transfer, or through Upwork or Fiverr if you prefer their escrow.
